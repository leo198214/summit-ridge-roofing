# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners evaluating roofing repair, replacement, or inspection and looking for a clear next step.

## Product Purpose

Summit Ridge Roofing presents a residential roofing service and guides visitors toward requesting an assessment.

## Positioning

Clear assessment and practical recommendations are the confirmed service promise; the site should help visitors make a considered roofing decision.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically view the marketing site on desktop or mobile while researching a home project.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a responsive React landing page, deployed through GitHub Pages. It must remain usable on mobile and desktop and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Summit Ridge Roofing. Voice: calm, direct, and considered.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied aerial house video at `public/roofing-aerial.mp4` and the local roof image at `public/roof-hero.webp` are approved visual assets. No testimonials, benchmarks, or pricing claims are supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make the next step clear.
- Show the quality and context of residential roofing work.
- Keep the experience confident, useful, and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and a non-animated visual fallback for reduced-motion preferences.
